# 004 Cel Shader and Outline

## Summary
Give opted-in models a cel/toon look matching the owner's Blender reference: four hard-edged colour bands driven by a single camera-fixed light from the upper right, colours taken from the model's own base colour texture (except a flat light-spot colour), plus a thick solid screen-space outline drawn by a post-process pass on opted-in models only. Toon shading and outline are a generic, opt-in capability of `Model` (or a thin wrapper); the moon (`src/assets/3D/moon.glb`) is the first consumer. `Model` without the opt-in behaves exactly as in 003. The fallback poster `src/assets/hero-planet-poster.png` is regenerated from the cel-shaded moon. All 003 guarantees keep holding unless explicitly amended below. 003 deferred this work ("Cel/toon shader, outlines, post-processing (next spec)").

Brownfield on 003: Vite 8, React 19 (StrictMode on), `three` 0.186.1, `@react-three/fiber` 9.8.2, `@react-three/drei` 10.7.9. One page-tall orthographic canvas (`src/three/PageCanvas.jsx`, `flat` = no tone mapping, alpha, antialias, DPR set by `DprController`) renders stars (`StarLayer.jsx`) and the moon (`PageScene.jsx` -> `Moon.jsx` -> `Model.jsx`). The moon material today is the GLB's own `KHR_materials_unlit` material (base colour texture "Moon_BaseColor"), with no lights in the scene. Dev poster capture: `src/three/PosterCapture.jsx` via `/?capture-poster`.

## Reference (owner, Blender viewport screenshot)
Grey cratered moon on a dark background. Hard-edged cel bands, no gradients: a bright flat-ish highlight patch upper right, the plain texture tone across the middle, a darker band, and a darkest band toward the lower left. Light comes from the upper right. Thick solid black outline around the silhouette (visibly heavier on the shadowed lower-left side; the owner chose a uniform thickness instead, D-12). Craters read through the bands via their geometry normals. Crater rims are NOT fully outlined: only partial black strokes on the side of a crater facing away from the light define rough crater shapes; the lit side of a rim has no stroke (D-16). The silhouette outline is one solid, continuous black band with hard edges (D-15). All visual checks compare against `specs/004-cel-shader/reference.png` (D-18).

## Goals
- The moon on `/` and in the poster looks like the reference: 4 hard bands, camera-fixed upper-right light, texture-derived colours, thick black silhouette outline.
- Toon shading and outline are reusable, per-model opt-in features; any `.glb` can use them with no change to shared code.
- All tunables (band colours/multipliers, thresholds, light direction, outline colour/thickness/inner-edge mode) are named constants in one place.
- 003 guarantees (lazy chunk, fallbacks, context loss, disposal, StrictMode, reduced motion, DPR/pixel budget, no third-party requests) still hold.

## Non-goals
- Bloom, glow, rim light, specular, shadows (cast or received), environment maps, any post effect other than the outline.
- Outlining or toon-shading the starfield or anything not opted in.
- Inverted-hull outlines (owner chose screen-space post-process, D-5).
- Normal-map authoring or baking; changes to `moon.glb` itself.
- A runtime UI or `/viewer` controls for tuning; tuning is by editing constants.
- Changes to layout, copy, Navbar, sections, GSAP/Lenis, star motion, moon spin speed, pointer parallax magnitudes, framing (003 FR-16/FR-30) or the canvas sizing/DPR logic beyond what the outline needs to read.
- Transparent/alpha-blended or alpha-tested materials under toon shading (see Assumptions).

## Decisions
### Owner decisions
- D-1 Colour ramp (owner): cel shading follows a Blender ColorRamp with constant interpolation: 4 hard bands, brightest to darkest:
  - Light spot: flat hard-coded colour `#EDEDED` (not derived from the texture).
  - Lit: base colour × 1.0.
  - Shadow 1: base colour × 0.67.
  - Shadow 2: base colour × 0.33.
  "Base colour" always comes from the model's own material (base colour texture, see FR-4); only the light spot is a fixed colour.
- D-2 Band thresholds and light direction (owner): the planner matches the reference as closely as possible (D-8, D-9); the owner will tweak the values manually later, so they must be clearly named, grouped, documented tunable constants in one obvious place (FR-9).
- D-3 Light direction (owner): a single directional term, fixed relative to the camera, pointing from the upper right of the screen as in the reference. Spin moves surface features through the bands; pointer tilt of the moon rig also shifts the shading. No three.js light objects are required unless plan.md justifies one.
- D-4 Outline technique (owner): post-process screen-space edge detection, chosen over inverted hull.
- D-5 Outline scope (owner): the outline applies only to models the owner opts in (per model), never to the starfield. Toon shading and outline are separate opt-ins.
- D-6 Reusable opt-in (owner): toon shading and outline are generic capabilities exposed through `Model` (props) or a thin wrapper around it; the moon is the first consumer. `Model` without the opt-in behaves exactly as today. `src/assets/3D/README.md` is updated.
- D-7 Poster (owner): `src/assets/hero-planet-poster.png` is regenerated from the cel-shaded, outlined moon via the existing dev capture flow so the fallback matches the live scene.

- D-10 Colour math space (owner, delegated to the orchestrator): multipliers (1.0 / 0.67 / 0.33) are applied to the base colour in LINEAR space, as Blender's shader nodes do. `#EDEDED` is an sRGB display value and must appear on screen as exactly `#EDEDED` (converted to linear for shading; output with the canvas's existing sRGB output and no tone mapping). Shadow 2 therefore displays at about 0.61 of lit in sRGB brightness. Matching the reference on screen assumes Blender's "Standard" view transform (Assumptions).
- D-11 Band-edge anti-aliasing (owner approved): band transitions may be smoothed over at most 1 device pixel in screen space (derivative-based) to stop crawling/shimmer as the moon spins. Bands must still read as hard edges (no visible gradient at 100% zoom). Named constant to turn it off (width 0).
- D-12 Outline (owner; AMENDED by D-15, D-16, D-17, which supersede any conflicting text here, incl. "inner edges such as crater rims" as full outlines and any anti-aliased outline edge): colour pure black `#000000`, opaque; thickness 3 CSS px default, multiplied by the renderer's effective pixel ratio (DPR after `DprController`, not raw `devicePixelRatio`); UNIFORM thickness all around (no heavier shadow side); drawn outside the silhouette (does not cover moon pixels). Scope: silhouette AND inner edges such as crater rims, if feasible. The plan chooses the edge-detection signal (e.g. depth and normal discontinuities) and the thresholds as tunable constants; if inner edges prove infeasible within budget, plan.md must say so and fall back to silhouette-only, flagged. Inner-edge mode is a boolean constant. All named constants.
- D-13 Dependencies (owner: "do whatever is easier"): the plan may choose either a custom outline pass built from `three`/R3F (already installed) or the `postprocessing` / `@react-three/postprocessing` packages, whichever is simpler to implement correctly with per-object opt-in, crater-rim edges, disposal, context loss and the bounded-cost rules (D-14). Any new package must be pinned to an exact version compatible with the pinned R3F/three/React versions (peer-range check like 003 D-5), and the choice is justified in plan.md.

### Owner decisions after layer 3 was built (feedback; supersede earlier conflicting text)
- D-15 Solid silhouette outline (owner): the outer outline is SOLID black from the silhouette outward: no antialiased/alpha fade at its outer edge (no gray fringe) and no gray or moon-coloured gap between the moon body and the black band. Thickness stays 3 CSS px, uniform. Alpha is binary (0 or 1) in the outline band. Observed defect at 1000x900 DPR 1 scanning outward over the right limb: black (inner-edge line), 153 lit (moon colour strip), black (ring), alpha 127 (fade), transparent. Wanted: moon colour, then one continuous black band, then transparent. Inner strokes must therefore never sit within the outline thickness of the silhouette, and the ring edge is a hard cut. The moon body's own edge keeps the MSAA blend of 003 (moon over black), the only intermediate-alpha pixel allowed at the boundary.
- D-16 Crater strokes only on the shadow side (owner): inner-edge strokes are kept ONLY where the surface at the edge faces away from the camera-fixed light (`CEL_LIGHT_DIRECTION`, view space); the lit side of a rim gets no stroke, giving partial strokes that define rough crater shapes as in `reference.png`. How strict the gating is, is a named, documented tunable constant (a facing threshold on N.L). The planner decides how N.L is computed (plan D-D.4).
- D-17 Stroke edges stay hard (owner did not ask for soft edges): inner strokes are solid black with binary alpha (no smoothstep ramp), matching the cel look. Any smoothing needs planner justification and a flag; the plan keeps none (risk noted).
- D-18 Visual checks (owner): every visual verification of outline, strokes and bands compares side by side against `specs/004-cel-shader/reference.png`.
- D-19 Grazing-angle gate for inner strokes (owner, after browser finding; amends D-15/FR-23): a pixel scan straight down through the bottom limb (1000x900, DPR 1) showed moon colour, a 10-20 px black run, a 6 px strip of moon colour, a 3 px black ring, then transparent. Cause: the inner-edge detector fires on the limb itself because view-space normals change fast near the silhouette; the D-15 silhouette margin (2 x thickness) only discards strokes within 6 px of the edge, leaving the gray strip. Fix: no inner stroke where the surface is at a grazing angle to the camera, i.e. where the reconstructed view-normal z at the pixel is below a named tunable constant `OUTLINE_INNER_GRAZING_Z` (default 0.35, range [0, 1]). The silhouette margin stays. The ring alone forms the limb edge.
- D-20 Silhouette only, inner edges off (owner, after seeing the result; supersedes the inner-edge parts of D-12, D-16, D-17, D-19): keep ONLY the solid black silhouette outline; no crater/inner-edge strokes. The inner-edge code (T-16, T-19, T-36, T-37, T-38) stays in the codebase as a dormant path, switched off by `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES = false` in `src/three/celConstants.js`; re-enable later by setting it to `true`. D-12 (inner-edge scope), D-16 (shadow-side gating), D-17 (hard stroke edges) and D-19 (grazing gate) remain documented as that dormant path and apply only when `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES` is `true`. The silhouette rules (D-12 colour/thickness/uniform/outside, D-15 solid binary band) are unchanged and active. Active requirement: no lines inside the disc (FR-27).

### Planner proposals (owner tweaks values later)
- D-8 Light direction default: view-space direction toward the light `normalize(1, 1, 1)` (x right, y up, z toward the viewer). Named constant.
- D-9 Band thresholds default (matching the reference as closely as the planner can; owner tweaks later), on `NdotL = dot(N, L)` in [-1, 1] with N the view-space shading normal:
  - Light spot: `NdotL >= 0.85`
  - Lit: `0.30 <= NdotL < 0.85`
  - Shadow 1: `-0.20 <= NdotL < 0.30`
  - Shadow 2: `NdotL < -0.20`
  With D-8 this gives a light spot in the upper-right quadrant, lit across the centre (disc centre NdotL ≈ 0.58), and Shadow 2 at the lower-left limb (NdotL ≈ -0.82), matching the reference. Named constants.
- D-14 Outline cost bound: because the canvas is page-tall (003 D-14, up to the 003 NFR-6 pixel budget), outline work and any offscreen render target are confined to the opted-in objects' screen region (plus outline thickness) or at most the visible viewport area; never a full page-tall pass. Outline work is skipped entirely while no outlined object is visible (moon offscreen per 003 FR-33).

## Users/Use cases
- Visitor on `/` sees the moon cel-shaded with a bold black outline, bands sweeping across craters as it spins and shifting slightly with pointer tilt; stars look unchanged.
- Visitor in any fallback (reduced motion, no WebGL, model/shader failure, context loss) sees the poster, which now shows the same cel-shaded, outlined moon.
- Owner adds a model and opts it into toon shading and/or outline with props, without touching shared shader or outline code.

## Functional requirements

### Opt-in and reuse
- FR-1: `Model` (or a thin wrapper in `src/three/`, named in plan.md) accepts per-instance opt-ins for toon shading and for outline, independently. Prop names and shapes are defined in plan.md. Defaults: both off. (D-5, D-6)
- FR-2: With no opt-in, `Model` renders exactly as in 003: same material instances as loaded from the GLB, no outline work, no extra render targets, materials, passes or shader programs created. (D-6)
- FR-3: Toon shading and outline code contain no moon-specific logic (no moon URL, texture name or moon constants). The moon opts in from `Moon.jsx` only. (D-6)

### Toon shading
- FR-4: For an opted-in model, every mesh is shaded with the 4-band ramp of D-1. Base colour = the material's base colour texture (sampled as sRGB, decoded to linear) multiplied by its base colour factor; if a material has no base colour texture, its base colour factor alone. Texture UVs, wrapping, flip and orientation stay as loaded (no mirrored/upside-down texture). Double-sidedness is preserved. (D-1, D-10)
- FR-5: The band is selected from `NdotL` against the thresholds of D-9, using constant (step) interpolation; band edges may be anti-aliased only as allowed by D-11. The light-spot band outputs the fixed colour of D-1 regardless of texture. (D-1, D-2, D-9, D-11)
- FR-6: N is the mesh's geometry (vertex) normal transformed to view space, so crater relief from the geometry shows through the bands. If a model provides a glTF normal map, the plan states whether it is used (default: used if present). (Reference)
- FR-7: L is the camera-fixed direction of D-8, constant in view space. Moon spin and pointer tilt change shading because the mesh rotates relative to L; resizing, scrolling and DPR changes do not change the shading. No three.js light object is added unless plan.md justifies it. (D-3)
- FR-8: Toon shading ignores `KHR_materials_unlit` on opted-in models (the opt-in means "shade with the ramp"). The original loaded materials are not mutated, so other non-opted consumers of the same cached GLB, and later remounts, still see the original materials. (D-6, 003 FR-18)
- FR-9: All ramp constants live in one module in `src/three/` as named exports, grouped and documented with short comments (meaning, unit, effect of raising/lowering): light-spot colour, three multipliers, three thresholds, light direction, band-edge AA width. Changing a constant changes the look with no other code edit. This module is the one obvious place the owner edits to tweak the look (D-2).

### Outline
- FR-10: Opted-in models get a screen-space outline produced by a post-process edge-detection pass over a per-object mask (or equivalent), so only opted-in objects contribute edges. Stars, background and non-opted models are never outlined and never trigger edges (e.g. a star behind the moon edge does not create or break an outline). (D-4, D-5)
- FR-11: Outline appearance follows D-12 constants: colour, thickness in CSS px scaled by the effective renderer pixel ratio (so on-screen thickness is the same CSS width at DPR 1, 1.5 and 2), placement outside the silhouette, uniform thickness, colour `#000000`, and inner-edge mode (ON by default: strokes at crater rims and other strong depth/normal discontinuities inside the disc, with the same thickness and colour, but only on the shadow side per FR-24 and never within the outline thickness of the silhouette per FR-23; if infeasible within budget, plan.md says so and the default becomes silhouette-only, flagged). Edge signal and thresholds are named constants. Constants live in the module of FR-9 (or a sibling module) as named exports.
- FR-27: No inner lines (D-20, ACTIVE). With `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES = false` (the default), no inner-edge material or quad is created and no black line appears inside the disc (no crater, rim or band-boundary strokes); the only outline is the silhouette band. FR-24, FR-25 (stroke part) and FR-26 apply only when `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES` is `true` and are N/A while it is off; the inner-edge parts of FR-11 are likewise dormant.
- FR-23: Solid outline (D-15). The silhouette outline is one continuous black band, `OUTLINE_THICKNESS_CSS_PX` x effective DPR device px wide (+-1), starting at the moon body edge and going outward, with binary alpha: every outline pixel is `#000000` at alpha 1 and the next pixel outward is alpha 0 (no intermediate alpha, no gray fringe). No moon-coloured or gray pixels may lie between the body edge and the band. Inner strokes are suppressed within a named margin (multiple of the outline thickness) of the silhouette so they cannot form a separate line inside the body edge.
- FR-24 (N/A while `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES` is false, D-20): Shadow-side crater strokes (D-16). With inner-edge mode on, an inner stroke is drawn only where the surface at the edge pixel faces away from the light: view-space N.L at that pixel below a named threshold constant (documented in `celConstants.js`: range [-1, 1], what raising/lowering does, default in plan.md). Lit-side rims get no stroke. A boolean constant can disable the gating (all rims stroked).
- FR-26 (N/A while `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES` is false, D-20): Grazing-angle gate (D-19, extends FR-23). Inner strokes are never drawn where the pixel's reconstructed view-normal z (`sqrt(max(0, 1 - |xy|^2))` from the mask normal xy) is below `OUTLINE_INNER_GRAZING_Z` (documented in `celConstants.js` with a "what to change" hint: raise = strokes keep further from the limb, lower = strokes allowed closer; default 0.35, number in [0, 1]). Together with the silhouette margin, the bottom-limb scan shows no black run or moon-colour strip inside the ring (AC-8c).
- FR-25 (N/A while `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES` is false, D-20): Hard stroke edges (D-17). Inner strokes are solid `#000000` with binary alpha (no soft ramp). Silhouette and strokes share the same colour and thickness.
- FR-12: The outline is in the same frame as the object it outlines (no one-frame lag during spin, tilt or scroll) and follows the object's silhouette exactly as it moves, including when partly clipped by the viewport edge.
- FR-13: Compositing on the transparent canvas: outline pixels are opaque in the canvas output (alpha 1 across the whole band, D-15) so they show over the page background; areas without moon or outline keep today's transparency and today's star rendering. The moon body keeps antialiased silhouette edges comparable to 003 (enabling the outline must not make the moon edge visibly more aliased). The outer edge of the black band is a hard cut (amended by D-15; it is no longer anti-aliased). (003 FR-5)
- FR-14: Cost bounds per D-14: outline work and render targets are limited to the outlined objects' screen region (plus thickness) or at most the viewport; no full page-tall offscreen buffer is allocated; outline work is skipped while no outlined object is visible (moon offscreen, 003 FR-33). Offscreen buffers are resized on canvas size/DPR change without per-frame allocation. (003 FR-8, NFR-6)

### Moon and poster
- FR-15: `Moon.jsx` opts the moon into toon shading and outline. Framing, spin, tilt, placement and visibility behaviour from 003 (FR-15, FR-16, FR-30, FR-33) are unchanged; the moon body's on-screen diameter (excluding outline) still meets 003 FR-16.
- FR-16: The poster is regenerated through the dev capture flow (`/?capture-poster`, dev-only, dead code in production) from the cel-shaded, outlined moon: 1200x1200, transparent background, no stars, default pose. The outline thickness in the poster is scaled so that, when the poster is displayed at its hero size at 1440x900, it matches the live outline within 1 CSS px. The provenance comment in `Hero.jsx` is updated to the capture date (the only allowed `Hero.jsx` edit). (D-7, 003 FR-25)

### Robustness (003 guarantees carried over)
- FR-17: Shader compile/link errors of the toon material or outline pass (via the existing `gl.debug.onShaderError` hook in `CanvasGuard.jsx` or equivalent) and any failure creating render targets result in `'fallback'` (poster, plain background elsewhere), with no uncaught errors. (003 FR-11, FR-36)
- FR-18: Context loss/restore: on loss the loop pauses and status is `'fallback'` as today; on restore the toon materials, outline pass and render targets are recreated or re-uploaded and the scene resumes with correct shading and outline, or stays in fallback. (003 FR-9)
- FR-19: Disposal: on unmount (route change to `/viewer`, StrictMode double-mount), every toon material, generated texture (e.g. ramp lookup, if any), outline material, full-screen geometry and render target created by this spec is disposed; `renderer.info.memory` and program count return to baseline. The original GLB materials/textures follow 003 FR-21 refcounted disposal unchanged. (003 FR-13, FR-21)
- FR-20: No per-frame allocation in any new `useFrame` callback or render pass (uniform updates write into preallocated objects). (003 FR-8)
- FR-21: Reduced motion, lazy chunk and network rules unchanged: reduced motion mounts no canvas (poster only); all new code is in `src/three/` behind the existing lazy boundary; no request to any third-party origin. (003 FR-2, FR-12, FR-22)

### Docs
- FR-22: `src/assets/3D/README.md` documents the opt-ins (props and an example), what toon shading uses from a model (base colour texture/factor, normals), the constants module and its defaults, that the outline is per model, and that non-opted models are used as exported. The "Materials are used as exported" note is amended accordingly. (D-6)

## Assumptions
- `moon.glb` provides vertex normals and UVs (003 README export settings). Whether it contains a normal map is not assumed; FR-6 default covers both.
- The base colour texture is tagged sRGB by the glTF loader (glTF spec); the canvas keeps sRGB output and no tone mapping (`flat`), so `#EDEDED` displays exactly.
- Toon opt-in targets opaque materials; transparent, alpha-blended or alpha-masked materials under toon shading are out of scope.
- The ramp constants are shared defaults; per-model overrides of ramp values are not required in this spec (plan may allow them if cheap).
- Outline sits on top of stars behind it; on the dark `bg-ink` page the black outline mainly reads where it covers stars and against the lit moon, as in the reference (dark background).
- Poster outline scaling (FR-16) uses the poster's displayed size at 1440x900 as the reference size.
- The moon remains the only toon/outlined model on `/` in this spec.
- The reference was rendered with Blender's "Standard" view transform (no Filmic/AgX); on-screen matching assumes this.
- Inner-edge outlines are assumed feasible from the moon's geometry normals and depth; D-12 defines the flagged fallback.

- D-20: `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES` is `false` by owner decision; the inner-edge assumptions below are dormant until it is set to `true`. Shimmer notes then concern the ring and band edges only.
- Hard binary stroke and ring edges may look stair-stepped or shimmer slightly during spin (NFR-4); no smoothing is planned (D-17). If T-23 judges shimmer unacceptable, the orchestrator flags it to the owner (options: 1 device px alpha ramp, or lower `OUTLINE_INNER_EDGES`).
- Shadow-side gating is by the stroke pixel's own shading (N.L), so strokes also appear on shadow-side rims of the unlit hemisphere only; per-crater "partial ring" shape follows from the edge signal combined with the N.L gate, as in `reference.png`.
