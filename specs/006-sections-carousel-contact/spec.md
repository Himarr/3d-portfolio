# 006 Section Redesign: Projects Carousel, Project Pages, About, Tech Stack, Contact and Footer

## Summary
Round 2 of the UX/UI pass (round 1 is spec 005, approved, not yet built; 006 is implemented after 005 and uses its snap, arrow, navbar and nested-scroller contracts). Projects, About and Tech Stack are redesigned, and a new Contact section is added as the last snap section. Each section is designed to fit exactly one screen at desktop sizes (no overflow, no clipping) and to flow normally below the 005 snap thresholds.
- Projects: the grid becomes an infinite carousel of image-first cards (centre card large, previous and next cards partly visible and dimmed). It auto-advances every 5 s, pauses on hover, keyboard focus, hidden tab and when out of view, is off with reduced motion, and supports prev/next buttons, dots, touch swipe, arrow keys and horizontal trackpad swipes. Vertical wheel and keys still snap sections (005). Clicking a card opens `/projects/:slug`, a bare placeholder page (with a not-found state).
- Project data moves to one shared data module used by the carousel and the routes.
- About: one screen, with a fixed-ratio image slot (placeholder until the owner supplies a photo or render), the bio and the facts list.
- Tech Stack: one screen, tools grouped by category, each with an owner-supplied icon (placeholder until then) and name, with an optional one-line use. Content comes from a data module.
- Contact (new, last): name, email and message form with accessible validation. Submit is not connected: a valid submit shows a "not connected yet" state and sends nothing. A footer inside the same screen holds the copyright line and GitHub and itch.io links.
- The navbar gains a "Contact" link (`/#contact`) on the desktop row and the mobile menu.

## Goals
- Every home section fills exactly one screen at desktop sizes and is built to fit (height-aware sizing, safe bands), not left to overflow.
- A projects carousel that looks image-first, loops forever, and is usable with mouse, touch, keyboard and assistive tech, without ever taking over vertical scrolling.
- Content (projects, tools, social links) lives in data modules the owner edits; images and icons are dropped in by filename.
- A contact form and footer ready for a backend, honest about not being connected.
- Everything carries over 005 (snap labels, arrow zones, nested-scroller marker, thresholds, reduced motion) and the site's tokens, fonts, focus styles and reveals.

## Non-goals
- Project page design (the route is a bare placeholder), project detail content, project filtering or categories.
- Form backend or any network request on submit, spam protection, email delivery, a form-service integration (a future spec).
- A 3D model in About (the image slot may later become a model in the fixed canvas), astronaut or section transition animations, a CMS.
- A play/pause button for the carousel (owner decision D-2).
- Mouse dragging of the carousel (touch swipe, wheel, keys and buttons only).
- A site-wide footer on other routes (footer is inside Contact on `/` only), a catch-all 404 route for arbitrary paths, a sitemap or SEO tuning, i18n.
- New dependencies (a carousel, form or icon library) and any change to the Hero or the 005 snap logic.
- Real content: text, links and images stay placeholders until the owner supplies them.

## Users/Use cases
- Visitor browses projects by looking at the large centre card, lets it auto-advance, or steers it with buttons, dots, arrow keys, swipe or a horizontal trackpad swipe, and opens a project page.
- Visitor hovers or tabs into the carousel to stop it moving.
- Visitor wheels or presses PageDown while the pointer or focus is on the carousel and still moves one section.
- Visitor on a phone swipes the carousel sideways and scrolls the page up and down normally.
- Visitor with reduced motion gets a static carousel they operate by hand.
- Visitor reads About and Tech Stack on one screen each, and sends a message (placeholder) from Contact, then sees the footer links.
- Owner edits a data module (projects, tools, social URLs) and drops in images and icons without touching components.

## Decisions (owner answers, treated as requirements; D-12 to D-17 are planner proposals, D-18 to D-23 are later owner answers)
- D-1 Projects become a carousel (centre card large, neighbours partly visible and dimmed, infinite loop, auto-advance every 5 s as a named constant, manual prev/next with owner-supplied icons, swipe, keyboard, indicators recommended).
- D-2 No play/pause button (owner). Autoplay pauses on hover and on keyboard focus within the carousel (D-21), when the document is hidden, and when Projects is not the current or visible section; it is off for `prefers-reduced-motion`; the 5 s timer restarts after any manual navigation. Trade-off recorded: WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide, Level A) asks for a mechanism to pause, stop or hide auto-advancing content that starts automatically and lasts more than 5 s. Hover and focus pause are not a visible control, so this design does not by itself meet the letter of 2.2.2 for people who neither hover nor focus (for example a screen-magnifier user, a voice-control user, or a user with attention difficulties). Mitigations kept: reduced-motion disables autoplay, off-screen and hidden-tab pause, slow 5 s pace, every control also operable by keyboard. Owner-accepted: the owner explicitly accepts the risk of having no visible pause control ("not that important in this case scenario"); this is no longer an open question. Reversible later by adding one button; no other requirement depends on its absence.
- D-3 Vertical input over the carousel always belongs to 005 snapping; the carousel never hijacks vertical scroll. A horizontal-dominant trackpad swipe over the carousel may change slides (FR-11).
- D-4 Project pages are a bare placeholder at `/projects/:slug` (FR-17, FR-18); design is out of scope.
- D-5 Project data lives in one shared module used by the carousel and the routes (FR-1). Placeholder projects are kept.
- D-6 Contact is the last snap section; the form is a layout only; a footer lives inside the Contact screen with GitHub and itch.io links (FR-21 to FR-27).
- D-7 The navbar gets a "Contact" link on desktop and in the mobile menu (FR-28).
- D-8 About gets a fixed-ratio image slot with a placeholder (FR-19). Tech Stack is grouped by category with owner-supplied icons and placeholders (FR-20).
- D-9 Submit is not connected: valid submit shows a "not connected yet" state; nothing is sent (FR-24).
- D-10 Owner supplies carousel prev/next icons, tech logos, GitHub and itch.io icons, the About image and project images; placeholders are used until then (Assets).
- D-11 Inherited from 005, restated as requirements: one screen per section without clipping, snap markers and labels, clear arrow zones, nested-scroller marker, behaviour below the snap thresholds, tokens/focus/reveal/reduced-motion carry over (FR-29 to FR-34).
- D-12 `kind` is dropped from project data and UI: the owner's card has an image, a title and an optional description only, and nothing else uses it. Re-adding it later is a one-field change.
- D-13 Icon strategy follows 005 D-2: single-colour UI icons (carousel prev and next, GitHub, itch.io) are plain SVG files tinted by `currentColor` (loaded through a CSS mask or equivalent that keeps `currentColor` states, no new dependency). Tech logos may be multicolour and are shown untouched by default; a per-tool `tint` flag in the data module renders a single-colour logo with `currentColor` for logos that are too dark for the dark theme.
- D-14 Height-aware sizing applies only where the 005 snap layout is on (`data-snap-layout="on"`). Below it, sizes are width-driven with sensible minimums and the page may grow taller than the screen.
- D-15 The Contact textarea carries the 005 D-11 marker (`data-lenis-prevent` or `data-snap-ignore`) only while its content overflows (scrollable). With short content it carries no marker, so wheel over it still snaps sections. The carousel has no nested scroll area and no marker.
- D-16 Section order and labels: Hero, Projects, About, Tech Stack, Contact (`data-snap-label` values "Hero", "Projects", "About", "Tech Stack", "Contact"). Navbar order: brand, Home, Projects, About, Contact, 3D Viewer (Tech Stack has no navbar link, per 005).
- D-17 Safe bands: at snap sizes no text or interactive content sits within `SECTION_SAFE_BAND_PX` (72 px: 16 px arrow offset + 44 px arrow + 12 px clearance) of the top or bottom viewport edge. The exception is the Contact footer in the bottom band, because the down arrow does not exist on the last section. The band is full width (simpler than a centre-column exclusion).
- D-18 Side-card click (owner): clicking a dimmed side card only slides it to the centre and never navigates. Opening a project needs a click or Enter on the active (centre) card (FR-14).
- D-19 Dot indicators (owner): kept, with the "n / N" text counter fallback above `CAROUSEL_MAX_DOTS` (10) projects (FR-8).
- D-20 Error colour (owner): an `error` colour token is added to the `@theme` block in `src/index.css`, alongside the existing tokens. The plan picks the exact value so that it suits the palette (a muted, cool-compatible red or coral, not a saturated alarm red) and meets: error text on `ink` and on `surface` at least 4.5:1; error border and error icon against `ink` and `surface` at least 3:1. It replaces the `rim`-border default for invalid fields. It is used only for form validation states (error text, invalid border, optional error icon). Errors still use text plus `aria-invalid` plus `aria-describedby`, never colour alone (FR-23). This amends 005 NFR-7 and 002 FR-1 ("no new colours") for this one token.
- D-21 Focus pause (owner, amends FR-6): autoplay pauses only for keyboard focus inside the carousel region, meaning focus that matches `:focus-visible` (the browser's focus-visible heuristic: Tab or arrow-key focus, not focus caused by a mouse click). A mouse click on prev, next or a dot does not leave autoplay paused; the click is manual navigation and restarts the full 5 s interval. Hover pause is unchanged. Tests may use Tab and mouse clicks.
- D-22 Side-card reveal and bounded gap (owner, amends FR-3, FR-15 and AC-6): a named constant `CAROUSEL_GAP_MAX_PX` (64) bounds the gap between the centre card and the side cards. On wide or short windows, where the height-capped centre card would otherwise leave a larger gap, the side cards reveal more than the standard peek, up to the point where they are fully visible. Side cards stay dimmed and scaled (`CAROUSEL_SIDE_OPACITY`, side scale). If the window is so wide that fully visible side cards still leave a gap above `CAROUSEL_GAP_MAX_PX`, the gap grows only by that remainder.
- D-23 Tech Stack use-line size (owner exception to the 16 px text minimum): the optional one-line `use` text of a tool tile may be 14 px at snap-active sizes (`data-snap-layout="on"`) and is 16 px otherwise. The exception covers that line only; all other body text keeps the 16 px minimum.

## Functional requirements

### Project data
- FR-1: One data module (proposed `src/data/projects.js`) exports the ordered project list used by both the carousel and the `/projects/:slug` route (no second copy). Each project: `slug` (required, unique, lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, used in the URL), `title` (required, non-empty), `description` (optional, short), `image` (optional `{ src, alt, width, height }`; `alt` non-empty, `width` and `height` positive integers). `kind` is not part of the model (D-12). The three existing placeholder projects are kept (their titles and descriptions) with slugs `project-one`, `project-two`, `project-three`, and no image (placeholder shown). Each `image` entry carries a provenance comment next to it (source, author, licence, or "own work", per 002 FR-33). A dev-time check (script or assertion) fails on a duplicate or invalid slug, an empty title, or an image missing `alt`, `width` or `height`.

### Projects carousel
- FR-2: Card content. Each card shows, in order top to bottom: the image (the largest element of the card; fixed aspect `CAROUSEL_IMAGE_ASPECT`, `object-cover` so any owner image is cropped to the frame, `width`/`height` attributes from data, descriptive `alt`, `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`), then the title (`h3`, wraps, at most two lines visible), then the optional description (at most two lines visible; clamped text stays fully in the DOM). When `description` is absent no element and no empty gap is rendered. When `image` is absent, or the image fails to load, the existing gradient placeholder shows (decorative: `aria-hidden`, no alt) with the same frame size and no layout shift. Card borders, surface and hover/focus colours use theme tokens.
- FR-3: Layout. The active (centre) card is large and horizontally centred in the carousel viewport. The previous and next cards sit partly visible at the left and right, dimmed (opacity at most `CAROUSEL_SIDE_OPACITY`) and scaled down, and both are cut by the viewport edge unless the window is wide enough for them to be fully visible (D-22). The carousel viewport spans the full section width (to the window edges), while the section heading stays in the normal content container. At 1024 px wide and above, the visible part of each side card is at least 15% of that card's width and may grow up to the whole card (D-22); the standard peek is about 40% of the card, and the reveal grows beyond it only when needed to keep the gap between the centre card and a side card at or below `CAROUSEL_GAP_MAX_PX` (fully visible side cards are the limit). At 375 px wide each side peek is at least 12 px wide and the centre card is fully visible. The carousel clips only horizontally, only on its own viewport element; nothing is clipped vertically (focus rings and hover scale of the active card stay visible).
- FR-4: Infinite loop. Next from the last project shows the first, and previous from the first shows the last, always moving in the direction of travel (the carousel never rewinds across all slides). At every moment exactly one slide is active and the side slots show its neighbours with no empty gap, including during and after rapid input. Slide changes animate with `CAROUSEL_TRANSITION_S` using `transform` and `opacity` only, and are instant with reduced motion. Edge cases by project count N: N = 0 renders an empty-state line and no controls; N = 1 renders one centred card with no side cards, no controls, no indicators and no autoplay; N = 2 loops correctly (the two side slots may show the same project), and any repeated slide used for the side slots is hidden from assistive tech and the tab order and is never counted in "n of N".
- FR-5: Autoplay. While autoplay is allowed (FR-6), the carousel advances forward by one slide every `CAROUSEL_AUTOPLAY_MS` (5000 ms, a named constant in one commented module; editing it is the only change needed to retime). The interval is measured from the start of each advance. Autoplay never runs when N is below 2.
- FR-6: Pause, resume and restart. Autoplay is paused (timer stopped, no advance) while any of these holds: the pointer is over the carousel region (card, side cards, buttons, indicators); keyboard focus (focus matching `:focus-visible`, D-21) is anywhere inside the carousel region (focus left on a button by a mouse click does not pause); `document.hidden` is true; less than `CAROUSEL_VISIBLE_RATIO` of the carousel is inside the viewport (so autoplay runs only while Projects is the current or visible section, including when the user is on another section or another route); `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` matches (autoplay is disabled entirely, no timer exists, also when the preference changes at runtime, with no reload). Every resume (hover ends, focus leaves, tab visible again, carousel back in view) starts a fresh full interval, not the remaining time. Any manual navigation (button, dot, swipe, arrow key, horizontal wheel, card click on a side card) restarts the full interval. Moving the pointer or focus onto a control never advances the slide by itself.
- FR-7: Prev/next buttons. Two `<button type="button">` controls with accessible names "Previous project" and "Next project" and `aria-controls` pointing to the slide track. Icons come from `src/assets/icons/carousel-prev.svg` and `carousel-next.svg` (placeholders until supplied, marked inside the file), decorative (`aria-hidden`), tinted with `currentColor`. Each has a hit area of at least 44x44 px and distinct hover, `:focus-visible` and active states using tokens (CSS transitions, `motion-reduce:transition-none`). They are visible on touch devices too, do not overlap the title, description or each other, and sit outside the 005 arrow safe bands (D-17). One activation moves exactly one slide; during a running transition a further activation is applied from the current state (so N quick clicks move N slides, never leaving a blank gap). Replacing either icon file with another valid `currentColor` SVG of the same name needs no code change.
- FR-8: Indicators. A group of one button per project (`aria-label` "Show project n of N: <title>", `aria-current="true"` on the active one, hit area at least 44x44 with a small visible dot, tokens only) placed below the card and outside the bottom safe band. Activating one shows that project (direction by shortest path in the loop) and counts as manual navigation. Dots follow autoplay and manual changes. With N above `CAROUSEL_MAX_DOTS` the dots are replaced by a text counter "n / N" (`aria-hidden`; the live region carries the news). Hidden when N is below 2. The dots are plain buttons, not a `tablist` (so 005 keyboard handling is not disturbed). Indicators ship (D-19).
- FR-9: Touch swipe. On touch and pen input, a horizontal swipe of at least `CAROUSEL_SWIPE_MIN_PX` that is dominantly horizontal (`|dx| >= CAROUSEL_SWIPE_AXIS_RATIO x |dy|`) over the carousel changes one slide (swipe left shows the next, right shows the previous). The carousel viewport sets `touch-action: pan-y` so vertical touch scrolling stays native; the code never calls `preventDefault` on touch events (005 FR-5, D-4); a cancelled gesture (the browser took it for scrolling) does nothing. A swipe never also opens a project. Mouse dragging is not implemented.
- FR-10: Keyboard. ArrowLeft and ArrowRight, while focus is inside the carousel region (a button, dot or the active card link), show the previous and next slide and count as manual navigation; they do nothing elsewhere on the page. Enter on the focused card link opens that project; Enter or Space on a button, dot activates it (native). Home, End, PageUp, PageDown, ArrowUp, ArrowDown and Space on non-button targets are not handled by the carousel and keep their 005 behaviour (section snap), even with focus inside the carousel. After a keyboard-initiated slide change, if focus was on the previously active card link it moves to the new active card link; if it was on a button or dot it stays there. Focus never falls to `<body>`.
- FR-11: Wheel and trackpad. Vertical-dominant or equal wheel input (`|deltaY| >= |deltaX|`) over any part of the carousel is not handled by the carousel (no `preventDefault`, no `stopPropagation`): with snapping active 005 moves one section; without it the page scrolls natively. Horizontal-dominant input (`|deltaX| > |deltaY|`, the same test 005 uses to "pass") with `|deltaX|` at least `CAROUSEL_WHEEL_MIN_DELTA` and no Ctrl key is handled by the carousel: it changes exactly one slide per gesture and calls `preventDefault` (so the browser's swipe-to-go-back gesture is not triggered). Trackpad inertia: after a slide change, further events of the same stream are ignored until the stream has been quiet for `CAROUSEL_WHEEL_GESTURE_GAP_MS` and the transition has ended, so one swipe never skips slides. The carousel adds no nested-scroll marker (D-15) and never blocks 005's capture-phase handler. Horizontal wheel counts as manual navigation. The listener is non-passive only on the carousel element, and is removed on cleanup.
- FR-12: Semantics. The carousel container is `role="region"` with `aria-roledescription="carousel"` and an accessible name (for example "Projects"). Each slide is `role="group"` with `aria-roledescription="slide"` and `aria-label` "n of N" (real position among N real projects). The slide track has `aria-live="off"` while autoplay is running and `aria-live="polite"` whenever autoplay is paused (hover, focus, hidden, out of view), disabled (reduced motion, N below 2) or after a manual change, so users are told the new slide only when they caused it or autoplay is not running. Prev/next buttons and dots follow FR-7 and FR-8. Heading order stays `h2` Projects then `h3` per card.
- FR-13: Focus and tab order. Only the active slide's interactive content is tabbable and exposed. Inactive slides (side cards and any repeated slides) are inert: removed from the tab order and from the accessibility tree (`inert`, or `aria-hidden` plus `tabindex="-1"` on descendants). Tabbing through the whole page visits exactly one card link per page pass. Tab order inside the region is logical and documented: previous, next, indicators, then the active card link (the plan may place the card link first if it documents why). No keyboard trap. The active card's link shows a visible focus ring around the whole card (3:1 on the dark background).
- FR-14: Card activation. The active card is one link target to `/projects/<slug>`: the title is a router link (accessible name = project title, not the description), and the whole card area (image, title, description) is clickable through that single link (no nested links, no duplicate tab stop). Click or Enter navigates without a full reload. Side (inactive) cards are not links; a pointer click on a side card brings it to the centre (manual navigation) and does not navigate (D-18: slide only, never navigate, also with Enter or touch tap; keyboard and assistive-tech users use the buttons, dots and arrow keys).
- FR-15: Fit. The Projects screen contains the eyebrow, heading, optional intro line, carousel (card, buttons) and indicators within the area between the safe bands. At snap sizes the card is height-aware (D-14): the image height is derived from the space left after the heading, title, description, indicators and gaps, never fixed; the centre image is at least 400 px wide at 1920x700, 1366x768, 1280x720, 1024x768 and 1024x700, and at least 700 px wide at 1920x1080 and 1440x900. Below the snap layout the card width is about the viewport width minus side peeks and gutters, with a minimum readable size, and the section may be taller than the screen.
- FR-16: Projects section identity. The section keeps `id="projects"`, the 005 markers (FR-31), its `aria-labelledby` heading (`SplitHeading` `h2`) and a one-shot reveal on the eyebrow, heading and intro line; the carousel container itself is revealed as a single block (FR-34), never its slides.

### Project pages
- FR-17: Route `/projects/:slug` renders a placeholder page inside the normal shell (navbar present): one `h1` with the project title, a line "Details coming soon." and a link "Back to projects" to `/#projects`. A slug that is not in the data module renders a not-found state instead: one `h1` ("Project not found"), a short message and the same back link, with no console error. Slug matching is exact and case-sensitive; a trailing slash is accepted. Project page design is out of scope (plain, token-styled, readable at 375 px and 1920 px, no horizontal overflow). The route is lazy or eager at the plan's choice, adds no canvas and no footer.
- FR-18: Navigation behaviour. Arriving at a project page from the carousel starts at the top of the page (`scrollY` 0), even though the user left `/` scrolled to Projects. Snapping, arrows and the settle are inactive there (005 FR-2, route is not `/`), and navbar links work from it: Home goes to `/`, Projects, About and Contact go to the matching section on `/` with the 005 scroll-to-section behaviour (FR-12/FR-13 of 005). "Back to projects" does the same as the Projects link (lands on Projects with its top at the viewport top within 1 px at snap sizes). Browser Back returns to `/` and the page rests on a section (005 FR-10 settle where snapping is active); the carousel starts again at the first project (its state is not persisted).

### About Me
- FR-19: About fits one screen (FR-29) and is re-laid out: the heading block (eyebrow "Background", `h2` "About Me"), the two bio paragraphs and the three facts (Focus, Based in, Open to; text unchanged) plus an image slot. The image slot is a frame with a fixed aspect `ABOUT_IMAGE_ASPECT`, `object-cover`, `width`/`height` attributes and a descriptive `alt` when an image is set, and a tokens-only gradient placeholder (decorative, `aria-hidden`) when not. The image object `{ src, alt, width, height }` lives in an obvious, commented place (data module or top-of-file constant) with a provenance comment; supplying it needs no layout change. At snap sizes the frame is height-aware (D-14): its height never exceeds the space between the safe bands, so it never forces the section past one screen; text and frame sit side by side from `md` up and stack below. Reveals use `data-reveal` on text blocks and the frame wrapper. The section keeps `id="about"`, the 005 markers and its heading.

### Tech Stack
- FR-20: Tech Stack fits one screen (FR-29). Tools are grouped by category from a data module (proposed `src/data/techStack.js`): `[{ id, label, tools: [{ id, name, icon?, use?, tint? }] }]`. Placeholder categories and tools, editable by the owner: Modeling (Blender, "Placeholder tool"), Game Dev (Unity), Web (Three.js, React) keeping the five current tools. Layout: each category is a labelled group (`h3` plus a list); categories sit side by side from `md` up and stack below. Each tool tile shows its icon (square slot, `object-contain`, `alt=""` because the name is adjacent text) and its name; `icon` is the imported asset URL for the owner's file at `src/assets/tech/<id>.svg`. ### Contact

- FR-22: Form fields. A `<form>` with `novalidate` (validation is presented by FR-23) containing: Name (`type="text"`, `autocomplete="name"`, required), Email (`type="email"`, `autocomplete="email"`, `inputmode="email"`, required), Message (`<textarea>`, required, fixed visible rows, `maxlength` `CONTACT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH`, `resize: none`, `autocomplete="off"`), and a submit button "Send message". - FR-23: Validation and accessible errors. The first submit attempt with any invalid field is cancelled: no data leaves, the "not connected" state (FR-24) does not show, each invalid field gets an inline error message directly under it (text, not colour alone; plain language that names the field and the problem, for example "Enter your name.", "Enter a valid email address.", "Write a message."), `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby` pointing at the error element, and focus moves to the first invalid field (no extra scroll beyond the browser's focus scroll). Validity uses the browser's constraint validation (`required`, `type="email"`, `maxlength`, `checkValidity`), plus a trim rule so a whitespace-only name or message is invalid. After the first attempt, each field re-validates as the user types or leaves it: its error clears as soon as it is valid and returns if it becomes invalid again. Errors are not announced twice (no `role="alert"` on each field; the focus move and `aria-describedby` carry them). Error styling uses the new `error` token (D-20) for the message text, the invalid field border (replacing the `rim` border) and an optional small icon, and otherwise existing tokens; the colour is never the only cue (text, `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` always present). The token is added in `src/index.css` `@theme` with a comment recording the chosen value and its measured contrast ratios.
- FR-24: Not-connected state. A submit with all fields valid is handled in JavaScript: `preventDefault`, no `action`/`method` navigation, no `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `sendBeacon`, `mailto:` or any other network or app launch, and no change to the URL (field values never appear in the address bar). A visible status block (`role="status"`, polite) says in plain words that the form is not connected yet and that nothing was sent (for example "This form is not connected yet. Nothing was sent."), and shows no success styling or confirmation wording. Field values stay in the fields. The status is replaced by nothing until the next edit of any field, which hides it; submitting again shows it again. The submit button stays enabled. Under reduced motion the status appears without animation.
- FR-25: Nested scroller. The message textarea carries `data-lenis-prevent` (or `data-snap-ignore`, 005 D-11) only while its content overflows its visible rows, and carries no marker otherwise; the marker is updated when the content or the size changes (in an input or `ResizeObserver` callback, never in wheel handlers). Keys typed in the name, email or message fields (including Space, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown and the arrows) never move the page (005 FR-4).

## Non-functional requirements
- NFR-1 Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA throughout, except the recorded 2.2.2 trade-off (D-2). Exactly one `h1` per page (Hero on `/`, the title on a project page).

- NFR-5 Theme: tokens only (`ink`, `surface`, `line`, `text`, `muted`, `accent`, `rim`, `focus`, fonts) plus the one new `error` token of D-20 (form errors only); no other new colour or font.

## Edge cases (required behaviour)
- Project count N of 0, 1, 2, and large (above `CAROUSEL_MAX_DOTS`): FR-4, FR-8.
- Rapid next/prev/dot activations, and a manual action during an autoplay transition: FR-7; the timer restarts from the manual action (FR-6) and no blank gap shows.
- Hover, focus or a hidden tab starting mid-transition: the transition finishes, then autoplay stays paused.
- Autoplay and window resize: recalculates sizes with no stuck transition and no jump to a wrong slide.
- Reduced motion switched on while the carousel auto-advances: autoplay stops immediately and the current slide stays. Switched off: autoplay may start again when conditions allow, with a fresh interval.
- Image missing, failed to load, or very different aspect ratio: placeholder or `object-cover` crop in the fixed frame, no layout shift (FR-2).
- Very long project title or description: wraps and clamps at two lines, full text stays in the DOM; no overflow.
- A swipe that starts on a card link: no navigation (FR-9). A click after a horizontal wheel gesture is a normal click.
- Vertical wheel with the pointer on the carousel, on a side card or on the buttons: always a section move (or a native scroll below the snap thresholds); the carousel never changes slide (FR-11). Diagonal input follows the dominant axis; an exact tie goes to vertical. Ctrl+wheel (browser zoom) is not handled.
- Slide change while a card link or button has focus: focus handoff per FR-10; focus never lands on `<body>`.
- Unknown slug, slug with trailing slash, project page opened directly or reloaded (the SPA fallback must serve it): FR-17.
- Navigating to a project page while scrolled down on `/` and returning with Back: FR-18.
- Contact submit with Enter inside a field, with autofill, with a whitespace-only value, with a message at the length limit: FR-22 to FR-24; double submit shows the same state; leaving the page with typed text has no prompt.
- Wheel over a textarea with and without overflow, and over the status block: FR-25.
- Footer links in a narrow window wrap into two rows with 44 px targets, no overflow (FR-26, FR-27).
- Browser zoom: thresholds are in CSS px (005), so zoom can turn snapping off or on; layouts re-fit (height-aware sizes follow the viewport), the carousel keeps working, and the page rests on a snap point after a re-align (005 FR-11).
- Navbar width: with the Contact link the desktop row is rechecked at the breakpoint (FR-28).
- StrictMode double effects: exactly one autoplay timer and one set of listeners (NFR-2).
